📓 The notebooks inside one box

Inside each box, pages are written into small notebooks called segments. The rules:

  • You can never rub anything out. New pages always go into a brand-new small notebook.
  • Deleting means crossing out. Click a page to give it a big ❌. It still takes up space!
  • Tidy-up time. Merging copies all the good pages into one neat notebook and throws the old ones away.

📦 Adding more boxes (shards)

You can't just add a box to an existing collection, because it would break the "page 42 goes in Box 2" rule. You have to split the boxes or move everything to a new collection.

Grown-up terms: Split is POST src/_split/target and needs a whole-number multiple (the index must be read-only first). Reindex is POST _reindex into a new index with any shard count. Replicas can be changed live with PUT my-index/_settings {"number_of_replicas": 2}. A common rule of thumb is roughly 10 to 50 GB per shard.
